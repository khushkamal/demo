Heritage & Architecture

A dialogue of stone, silence, and shadow

"We conceived a place of unhurried contemplation—where the architectural serenity of Ranakpur’s temples rests beside the untamed boulder hills of Jawai."

Constructed using hand-quarried Jodhpur yellow sandstone, Makrana white marble, and native Jawai granite, the retreat is designed as a low-slung, light-filtering sanctuary. Thick stone walls and handcrafted jaali screens temper the desert sun while framing unobstructed views of the Aravalli forest canopy.

Here, morning reveals mist drifting over stepwells, and twilight brings the quiet resonance of folk melodies across the courtyards under starry skies.

Traditional Craft

Lime-plaster finishes, hand-chiseled stone jharokhas, and Marwar woodwork shaped by local artisans.

Wild Stewardship

Deep harmony with the pastoral Rabari community and responsible safaris respecting the local leopard ecology.

Immersion & Exploration

The living tapestry of Southern Rajasthan

Thoughtfully curated private journeys designed to connect guests intimately with ancient architecture, wildlife, and pastoral traditions.

Wilderness

Jawai Leopard Expeditions

Custom open-top 4x4 safaris across ancient pre-Cambrian rock hills led by naturalists who have tracked these granite caves for decades.

*Note: Leopard and wildlife sightings are subject to natural movements in the wild and cannot be guaranteed.

Heritage

Ranakpur Temple at Dawn

A private early-morning walk through the 15th-century temple complex before public hours, observing how light illuminates 1,444 uniquely carved marble pillars.

Culture

Rabari Pastoral Culture Walk

Walk alongside nomadic Rabari elders through ancestral grazing paths, gaining insight into their peaceful coexistence with the wild leopards of Jawai.

Ecology

Jawai Dam Sunset & Birdlife

Afternoon tea by the expansive waters of Jawai Dam, watching flamingos, sarus cranes, and migratory birds along the granite shoreline.

Culinary

Stepwell Dining Under Starlight

Candlelit Rajasthani thali dining served on the stepwell terrace with ancestral Mewari recipes, accompanied by soulful Manganiyar instrumentalists.

Excursion

Kumbhalgarh Fortress Ridge

A scenic mountain drive to the UNESCO World Heritage fortress, standing atop the second-longest continuous fortification wall in the world.
