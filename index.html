Architecture & Landscape

Built from Jodhpur sandstone and Makrana marble.

The retreat sits in a quiet valley in the Pali district. Thick stone walls and hand-chiseled jaali screens keep the interiors cool during sunny afternoons.

Each building is oriented to catch the morning breeze from the forest hills. At sunset, the stone turns a deep amber.

Local Masonry

Lime plaster, yellow sandstone, and Sheesham woodwork crafted by regional artisans.

Wilderness Care

Guest Information

Frequently asked questions.

What is the best season to visit?

October through early April brings pleasant daytime temperatures (22°C–28°C) and cool starlit evenings. Migratory birds arrive at Jawai Dam from November onward.

Are leopard sightings guaranteed?

No. Leopard sightings depend entirely on natural wildlife behavior and cannot be guaranteed. Our local naturalists track territories carefully during morning and late afternoon drives.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Sample policy: Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early arrivals or late departures are subject to suite readiness on the day.

What is the cancellation policy?

Sample policy: Cancellations made up to 14 days before arrival receive a full refund. Cancellations within 14 days incur a 50% charge. In this demo prototype, no actual transactions occur.
