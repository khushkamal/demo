The Setting

Between granite boulders and forest valleys.

Aravalli Retreat rests in a quiet valley corridor between the forested hills of Ranakpur and the granite outcrops of Jawai. Days follow the movement of the sun, from morning drives tracking wild leopards to sunset chai served atop the warm stone ridges.

Built using local yellow sandstone and native granite, the lodge blends directly into the landscape. Open courtyards, shaded neem verandas, and stone walkways offer an undisturbed stay in rural Rajasthan.

Kitchen & Hearth

Wood-fire cooking and stepwell dinners.

Dishes rooted in regional Mewari recipes, cooked over slow wood embers with seasonal produce from local growers.

Baori Stepwell Dining

Dinner is served along the stone steps of the stepwell under lantern light. Slow-cooked lentils, wood-fired flatbreads, and regional winter specialties.

Terrace Mornings

Freshly pressed pomegranate juice, warm millet bread, farm eggs, and mountain honey served on the east-facing stone terrace following dawn drives.

Field Journeys

Guided outings into the wild.

Accompanied by resident naturalists and guides from neighboring villages.

Wildlife notice: Sightings are not guaranteed. Drives follow natural wildlife movements and ethical tracking protocols.

Jawai Leopard Safaris

Early morning drives across granite hills where leopards move across the open rock. Trackers use ancestral route knowledge to navigate the terrain.

Ranakpur Temple Walk

Morning walk through the quiet 15th-century forest temple, exploring its 1,444 sculpted marble pillars before public hours.

Rabari Pastoral Walk

Walk with Rabari herders along pastoral routes and observe their generational living beside the granite hills and wildlife corridors.

Jawai Dam Wetlands

Afternoon birdwatching by the lake shores, where migratory flamingos, cranes, and marsh crocodiles gather during winter months.
