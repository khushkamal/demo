The Experience

Days shaped by the wild and the hills.

Mornings begin early with chai before setting out across granite hills in an open 4x4 to track leopards. By midday, guests return for slow lunches under neem trees or read on private balconies overlooking the forest canopy.

Evenings bring tea by the reservoir as flamingos gather at sunset, followed by wood-fired dinners beside the candlelit stepwell. The buildings are constructed from local yellow sandstone and native granite.

Wood-fire cooking and stepwell dinners.

Simple food rooted in Mewari culinary heritage. Ingredients sourced from our kitchen garden and farms nearby.

Baori Stepwell Dining

Dinner is served along the stone tiers of the stepwell under lantern light. Expect clay-pot braised mutton, smoked dal bati with wild ghee, hand-pressed millet breads, and seasonal vegetable preserves.

Guest Information

Frequently asked questions.

What is the best season to visit?

October through early April brings clear skies and daytime temperatures between 22°C and 28°C, with cool evenings. Migratory birds arrive at the wetlands from November onward.

Are leopard sightings guaranteed?

No. Leopard sightings depend entirely on natural animal movements and cannot be guaranteed. Our trackers know the granite territories well and head out at the most active hours.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Sample policy: Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in is subject to availability on the day.

What is the cancellation policy?

Sample policy: Cancellations up to 14 days before arrival receive a full refund. In this demo prototype, no real transactions or cancellations occur.
