# Aravalli Retreat, Ranakpur–Jawai (Boutique Heritage Resort Demo)

An authentic, photography-led luxury demo website for a fictional heritage sanctuary located between Ranakpur and Jawai in Rajasthan, India. Designed with the calm, editorial reference aesthetic of **Amanbagh**, **Aman-i-Khás**, **SUJÁN Jawai**, and **The Oberoi Udaivilas** — 100% authentic high-resolution photography, warm alabaster linen tones, and cinematic scroll zoom & parallax motion.

> **DEMO ONLY NOTICE:** This website is an architectural design prototype and demonstration. All hotel names, room rates, guest reflections, and contact numbers are fictional placeholders. No real financial transactions or live bookings take place.

---

## 🏛️ Design & Visual Direction

- **No Low-Poly or Cartoon 3D**: Replaced all artificial low-poly blocks with 100% real high-resolution luxury resort photography (serene palace courtyards at dawn, carved marble, wilderness tents, stepwells, and wild leopards).
- **Cinematic Photography Parallax & Zoom**: Full-bleed photographs smoothly scale and drift (`scale: 1.1 -> 1.0`) on scroll with **Lenis** smooth scrolling and **GSAP ScrollTrigger**.
- **Palette**: Warm alabaster ivory (`#FAF7F2`), sandstone linen (`#F3ECE0`), muted terracotta accents (`#9E5A44`), deep charcoal-black typography (`#1C1917`), and dark forest olive contrast sections (`#181C17`).
- **Typography**: Editorial serif (*Cormorant Garamond* in light 300/400 weights) paired with refined sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Layout**: Magazine spreads, asymmetric grids, hairline dividers (`1px solid #E5DCCE`), and razor 0px square corners (no pill buttons).
- **Demo Safety**: `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`, clearly marked sample notes, obvious fictional contacts (`+91 00000 00000`), and a prototype badge.
